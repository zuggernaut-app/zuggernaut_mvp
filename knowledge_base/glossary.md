# Glossary of Google Ads Terms

*   **Account ID:** A unique number identifying a specific Google Ads account.
*   **Ad Extensions:** Additional information appended to ads (e.g., sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, image extensions) to enhance visibility, provide more context, and improve Click-Through Rate (CTR).
*   **Ad Group:** A collection of ads within a campaign that target a specific set of keywords or audiences.
*   **Attribution Modeling:** Assigning credit for conversions across multiple touchpoints in the customer journey. Google Ads offers various models (e.g., Data-Driven, Last Click).
*   **Automation & AI:** The increasing use of machine learning and artificial intelligence in Google Ads for campaign optimization (bidding, targeting, responsive ads, PMax campaigns).
*   **Automated Bidding Strategies:** Google Ads strategies that use AI to optimize bids for conversions or conversion value (e.g., Maximize Conversions, Target CPA, Target ROAS).
*   **Bidding Strategies:** Methods to control ad costs and achieve campaign objectives.
*   **Brand Awareness:** A campaign objective focused on increasing visibility and recognition of a brand.
*   **Calls:** A campaign objective focused on generating phone calls.
*   **Campaign:** The highest level of an ad account, grouping ad sets and ads that share a common objective and budget.
*   **Campaign Types:** Different formats for running ads (e.g., Search, Display, Video, Shopping, App, Performance Max).
*   **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition):** The average amount paid for each conversion.
*   **CPC (Cost Per Click):** The average amount paid for each click on an ad.
*   **CTA (Call to Action):** A prompt guiding users to take a specific action (e.g., "Shop Now," "Learn More").
*   **Conversion Tracking:** Measuring desired user actions (e.g., purchases, sign-ups) that result from ad clicks.
*   **Currency:** The monetary unit used for billing and reporting in an ad account.
*   **Display Ads:** Visual banner ads shown across the Google Display Network.
*   **Exclusive Inventory:** Ad placements unique to Google Ads (YouTube, Gmail, Maps, SERPs).
*   **First-Party Data:** Data collected directly from users (e.g., website visitors, CRM data), increasingly important due to privacy changes.
*   **GA4 (Google Analytics 4):** A web analytics service that tracks and reports website/app traffic and user behavior.
*   **Gmail Ads:** Ads appearing within Gmail inboxes.
*   **Google Tag Manager:** A tool for managing website tags and tracking codes.
*   **Implementation:** The stage of setting up and configuring ad campaigns.
*   **Leads:** A campaign objective focused on collecting potential customer contact information.
*   **Local Business Ads:** Ads appearing on Google Maps for local searches.
*   **Looker Studio:** A tool for creating custom data dashboards.
*   **Manual CPC (Cost Per Click):** Allows direct control over the maximum bid for each click.
*   **Measurement & Attribution:** Tracking performance and assigning credit for conversions.
*   **Native Integrations:** Seamless connections between Google Ads and other Google products.
*   **Online Sales:** A campaign objective focused on driving e-commerce transactions.
*   **Performance Max (PMax):** A highly automated, cross-channel campaign type using AI across all Google inventory to drive conversions.
*   **Platform Importance:** Google Ads' foundational role and transferable skills, with an evolving landscape heavily influenced by AI and privacy changes.
*   **Privacy Considerations:** Awareness of data privacy laws and their impact on advertising.
*   **Privacy Sandbox:** Google's initiative to create a more private environment for online advertising.
*   **ROAS (Return on Ad Spend):** A measure of profitability: (Revenue from ads / Cost of ads).
*   **Responsive Display Ads (RDAs):** Display ad formats that use AI to test combinations of headlines, descriptions, and assets.
*   **Responsive Search Ads (RSAs):** Search ad formats that use AI to test combinations of headlines and descriptions.
*   **Search Ads:** Text-based ads appearing on Google SERPs.
*   **SERP (Search Engine Result Page):** The page displayed after a Google search.
*   **Smart Campaign:** An automated, simplified campaign setup option (generally not recommended for experts).
*   **Strategy:** High-level planning and decision-making for advertising.
*   **Tactics:** Specific methods and approaches used to implement a strategy.
*   **Target CPA:** A bidding strategy aiming for a specific cost per conversion.
*   **Target ROAS:** A bidding strategy aiming for a specific return on ad spend.
*   **Traffic:** A campaign objective focused on driving website visitors.
*   **UAC (Universal App Campaigns):** A campaign type dedicated to promoting mobile apps.
*   **User Intent:** The active desire of a user to find something, indicated by their search queries.
*   **Video Ads:** Ads shown on YouTube and within apps.
