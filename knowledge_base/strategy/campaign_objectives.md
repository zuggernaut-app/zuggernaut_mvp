# Campaign Objectives

## Context
When an AI agent needs to determine the primary goal of an advertising campaign to guide subsequent strategic and tactical decisions.

## Core Concepts
*   **Traffic:** Drive website visitors.
*   **Leads:** Collect potential customer contact information.
*   **Calls:** Generate direct phone calls to the business.
*   **Online Sales:** Drive e-commerce transactions and product purchases.
*   **Brand Awareness:** Increase brand visibility and introduce new products/services broadly.

## Decision Rules
*   IF objective is "online sales" THEN prioritize tactics focused on direct conversion, potentially using Shopping or Performance Max campaigns.
*   IF objective is "lead generation" THEN prioritize tactics that capture contact information, often using Search Campaigns or Performance Max.
*   IF objective is "brand awareness" THEN prioritize tactics focused on reach and impressions, such as Display or Video campaigns.
*   **Cross-Objective Optimization:** Performance Max campaigns are often recommended for achieving multiple objectives efficiently due to their cross-channel nature and AI-driven optimization.
