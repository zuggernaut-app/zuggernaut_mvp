# Platform Overview

## Context
When an AI agent needs to understand the fundamental market position, unique advantages, and core principles of Google Ads to inform high-level strategic decisions.

## Core Concepts
*   **Market Dominance & Revenue**
    *   Google's search market share is over 90% (95% on mobile).
    *   Generated $237 billion in ad revenue in 2023.
    *   70% of Google's total revenue comes from Google Ads.
*   **Platform Importance**
    *   An early industry player, setting foundational concepts for other ad platforms.
    *   Skills learned are transferable across digital advertising.
    *   **Evolving Landscape:** Google Ads is continuously evolving, with a strong emphasis on AI, automation, and adapting to privacy changes.
*   **Exclusive Inventory**
    *   Unique ad placements available only through Google Ads:
        *   YouTube ads
        *   Gmail ads
        *   Google Play Store ads
        *   Google Maps ads
        *   Google Search Engine Result Pages (SERPs)
    *   Allows advertisers to reach users at specific points in their digital journey.
*   **User Intent**
    *   A key differentiator: users actively search for products/services, indicating high purchase intent.
    *   Contrasts with platforms where users are passively exposed to ads.
    *   Crucial for campaign success and targeting.
*   **Accessibility & Cost Model**
    *   Free to set up and access with a Gmail account.
    *   Advertisers typically pay on a Cost-Per-Click (CPC) or Cost-Per-Action (CPA) basis via bidding.
*   **Native Integrations**
    *   Seamlessly connects with:
        *   Google Analytics 4 (GA4): For post-click behavior analysis.
        *   Looker Studio: For custom dashboard creation.
        *   Google Tag Manager: For robust conversion tracking setup.
    *   Facilitates comprehensive measurement and reporting.
